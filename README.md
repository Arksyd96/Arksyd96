### 🔬 About My Work

- 🧠 **Research:** Author of multiple papers on generative models, notably **SBLDM** (Slice-Based Latent Diffusion Models) for 3D MRI volume synthesis in data-scarce regimes, and **AMM-Diff** for missing MRI sequence imputation.
- 🎯 **Current Focus:** Developing deep unlearning approaches for multi-center **PET image harmonization** in collaboration with Institut Curie, alongside orchestrating **NeuroTwin**, a multi-agent RAG system for medical report generation.
- 💼 **Background:** Ph.D. in Computer Vision applied to Medical Imaging (Univ. Rouen / Becquerel) • M.Sc. in Computer Vision (Univ. Paris-Descartes) • Ex-Data Scientist at Stellantis.
